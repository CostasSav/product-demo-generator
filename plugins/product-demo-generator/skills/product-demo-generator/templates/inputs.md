# Inputs — everything typed or chosen on screen

The director never invents an input. Every text typed, option picked, file uploaded or button
choice is written here first, scene by scene. When the app offers a choice with no exact match
below, the rule is: **the safest option** (no spending, nothing sent, anonymised when in doubt,
consistent with the scenario) — and the decision is logged in `decisions.md`.
